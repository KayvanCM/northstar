How Studies Work

Every new treatment climbs the same four steps.

Phase 1

Is it safe?

A small group tries it for the first time. The team looks for side effects and the right dose.

Usually 20 to 80 people
Phase 2

Does it work?

More people with the condition take it, to see if it helps and to keep checking safety.

Usually 100 to 300 people
Phase 3

Is it better?

It’s compared with the current standard treatment in a large group, often across many sites.

Usually 1,000 or more people
Phase 4

After approval

The treatment is available. Researchers keep watching how it does in everyday life.
